Detailed Description

template<cpt::number t_result, cpt::number t_number, std::size_t t_amount>
struct tnct::math::bus::moving_average< t_result, t_number, t_amount >

The moving average is computed from the accumulated sum divided by size, and the final value is converted to t_result. If t_result is integral, the fractional part is discarded by conversion.
